## Full description (max 4000)

```
Guitar Positions shows every scale, arpeggio and chord on the guitar fretboard, with fingerings you can play right away.

One purchase, no ads, no account, no tracking. The app works completely offline and never connects to the internet.

SCALES AND ARPEGGIOS
- 36 scales and 10 arpeggios in every key, from major and minor pentatonic to modes, bebop and symmetric scales
- Every position across the neck, as boxes or three notes per string
- Show intervals or note names, with theory-correct spelling (sharps, flats or your choice)
- Tap any note to hear it

CHORDS
- Playable chord shapes ranked by how easy they are to play, with fingerings
- Drop voicings from the Guitar Positions V-System
- Filter by bass note and neck region
- See which scales fit a chord and which chords belong to a scale

CHORD NAMER
- Tap a shape on the fretboard and get its name, alternative names and the scales that fit

SONGS AND EXERCISES
- Build chord charts with diagrams, keep them on your device
- Play a progression at your tempo
- Export scales, chords and songs as black-and-white A4 PDF sheets and share them

MADE FOR PRACTICE
- Favourites: save any view with one tap
- Haptic feedback when you tap the fretboard
- Alternate tunings plus ukulele, banjo and Loog Guitar (3 strings), 12 to 24 frets, left-handed mode, dark and light theme
- Horizontal and vertical neck, built for phones and tablets

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE
Songs, favourites and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no internet permission, collects nothing and contains no advertising.

Guitar Positions is open source (MIT License): https://github.com/hybridpicker/fretboard-position-finder
```
